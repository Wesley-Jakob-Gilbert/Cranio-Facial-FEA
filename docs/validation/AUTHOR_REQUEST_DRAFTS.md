## 1. Corresponding author, Herring pig strain papers (University of Washington)

Subject: Request for maxillary strain data from your minipig mastication work

Dear Professor Herring,

I am an independent researcher building an open finite element model of maxillary bone adaptation under daily loading, and I am planning a validation against measured strains. Your review "Jaw muscles and the skull in mammals" (Comp Biochem Physiol A, 2001) reports maxillary gauge strains in 11 minipigs, and your 2005 J Exp Biol paper reports zygomatic strains by food hardness.

Would you be willing to share, or point me to, the per-animal and per-cycle strain values for the maxillary sites (anterior, middle and posterior to the infraorbital foramen)? Gauge orientations, animal weights and any bite or muscle force estimates would help most. Aggregate tables are fine if raw traces are unavailable.

I will cite your work and credit the data in any publication, and I can share my model code and results in return. Thank you for considering this.

Best regards,
Wes Gilbert

## 2. Corresponding author, Macrì et al. (midpalatal suture density, 392 adolescents)

Subject: Request for aggregate CBCT density values, rapid palatal expander study

Dear Dr. Macrì,

I am developing an open model of adolescent maxillary bone density and would like to compare it with published observations. Your 2022 study of 392 adolescents with a rapid palatal expander reports midpalatal suture density at baseline and one year.

Could you share, or point me to, the mean and SD of bone density at T0 and T1 by age and sex, and the units or calibration used (HU or gray values)? De-identified aggregate values are sufficient.

I will cite the study and acknowledge your help. Thank you for your time.

Kind regards,
Wes Gilbert

## 3. Corresponding author, Boryor et al. (human skull RME in vitro)

Subject: Request for force-strain data, in vitro rapid maxillary expansion on human skulls

Dear Dr. Boryor,

I am working on an open finite element model of the maxilla and want to check it against experimental data. Your 2010 J Biomech paper compares zygomatic process strain, maxilla displacement and expansion force in fresh and Thiel-fixed human skulls with a finite element model.

Could you share the force-strain and force-displacement values for each specimen, along with specimen age and sex, and the material properties you used in the FE model? I will cite the paper and acknowledge your contribution, and I am happy to share the model and results.

Thank you very much,
Wes Gilbert
